# Emberwild — Early-Game Quest Map
## Hollowpath → Hearthcross → Mossroad Vale → Bridge Completion

> Purpose: Convert the current early-game concept into a usable quest-by-quest progression plan.
>
> Design goal: The player begins by learning how to survive, then learns how Emberwild's systems connect through rebuilding Hearthcross, and finally graduates into genuine regional exploration before repairing the Mossroad bridge and entering the wider world.

---

## 0. Structure at a Glance

1. Hollowpath — Survive
2. Hearthcross I — Keep Them Alive
3. Hearthcross II — Tools to Rebuild
4. Hearthcross III — Make It Safe
5. Hearthcross IV — Bring People Back
6. Mossroad Vale — Regional Recovery
7. Mossroad Bridge — Graduation

Guidance should evolve as:

**Instruction → guided problem solving → multiple active goals → regional freedom**

The player should gain broad literacy without being forced to master every profession.

---

## 1. Quest Type Legend

- **[MAIN]** mandatory starter-region story
- **[OPTIONAL]** skippable
- **[PROFESSION]** deeper skill progression
- **[CHARACTER]** NPC/personal story
- **[DISCOVERY]** exploration/item/event triggered
- **[RESTORATION]** physically advances Hearthcross
- **[CAPSTONE]** combines learned systems

---

## 2. Hearthcross Settlement States

### Stage 0 — Collapse
One usable communal shelter, broken palisade, empty storage, abandoned foundations, cold forge, few NPCs, no active shops.

### Stage 1 — Surviving
Food and medicine stabilized; cooking fire, stocked shelves, recovering villagers, crude healer corner.

### Stage 2 — Working
Basic timber, tools, workshop, and storage restored; lumber stacks, repaired benches, scaffolding, workers processing resources.

### Stage 3 — Defended
Perimeter and nearby resource routes safer; repaired palisade, guards, watchfires, road traffic.

### Stage 4 — Growing
Specialists and residents return; multiple structures complete, services unlock, profession mentors and traders appear.

### Stage 5 — Connected
Hearthcross influences Mossroad Vale; supply routes, stable economy, quarry/fishery/waystation connections.

### Stage 6 — Bridgeworks
Construction camp, bridge materials, scaffolding, work crews.

### Stage 7 — Restored Foothold
Bridge complete; thriving starter settlement, wider-world route open, optional upgrades remain.

# PHASE ONE — HOLLOWPATH
## Target: ~20–30 minutes

### Q00 — Second Life [MAIN]

**Problem:** Player wakes in an unknown forest with a partial System and no understanding of Emberwild.

**Objectives:**
- inspect disturbed ground
- trigger local System scan
- establish identity / Unbound status
- learn movement and interaction

**Teaches:** movement, interaction, System messages, objectives, environmental inspection.

---

### Q01 — What Was Left Behind [MAIN]

**Problem:** Player has nothing useful.

**Objectives:**
- search abandoned satchel/camp
- recover cordage, broken cutting tool/knife, cloth, simple restorative component
- inspect a sturdy branch

**Teaches:** containers, inventory, item categories, basic gathering.

**Change from prototype:** do not hand the player a finished combat weapon.

---

### Q02 — Make Do [MAIN]

**Problem:** Player needs a weapon before moving deeper into Hollowpath.

**Objectives:**
- gather sturdy branch
- combine branch with recovered materials
- craft **Crude Wooden Spear**

**Teaches:** crafting, recipe UI, resource consumption, equipment, weapon stats.

**Core lesson:** found materials can become something better.

---

### Q03 — Silverleaf [OPTIONAL] [PROFESSION]

**Trigger:** side-path exploration.

**Objectives:**
- gather Silverleaf
- inspect it
- combine herb + cloth/basic component into Field Poultice

**Teaches:** optional exploration, herb gathering, consumable crafting, healing.

If skipped, Rhea can explain restorative items later.

---

### Q04 — First Blood [MAIN]

**Enemy:** weak Mossling/equivalent.

**Teaches:** combat entry, attack, HP, consumables, loot, monster materials.

**Reward:** small XP, Moss Gel, System analysis entry.

---

### Q05 — Not Everything Wants You Dead [OPTIONAL] [DISCOVERY]

Approach passive Forest Stag.

**Teaches:** passive/neutral/aggressive behavior and System scanning.

---

### Q06 — Something Hunts Here [MAIN]

**Objectives:**
- inspect mauled animal
- inspect deep claw marks
- see fleeing wildlife
- hear distant growl
- receive failed System analysis

**Teaches:** environmental storytelling and level-inappropriate threats.

---

### Q07 — Smoke Through the Trees [MAIN]

**Objectives:**
- search wrecked camp
- discover Hearthcross reference
- inspect trail marker
- follow smoke east

**Teaches:** Journal updates, destination clues, map guidance.

---

### Q08 — RUN [MAIN]

Veilfang appears.

**Objectives:** flee toward the route to Hearthcross, survive chase, enter forced confrontation.

**Outcome:** narrative defeat; Rhea rescues player.

**Teaches:** threat warnings and that some fights cannot be won.

---

# PHASE TWO — HEARTHCROSS I: KEEP THEM ALIVE
## Stage 0 → Stage 1

### Q09 — Debt [MAIN] [CHARACTER]

Rhea establishes:
- she rescued the player
- scarce supplies were used
- Hearthcross is desperate
- the bridge is destroyed
- the town cannot currently repair it

The player is not imprisoned. Staying makes sense because they owe resources, lack knowledge, the region is dangerous, and Hearthcross is the safest option.

---

### Q10 — A Village in Name Only [MAIN] [DISCOVERY]

Player inspects:
- communal shelter
- empty food stores
- injured villagers
- ruined forge
- collapsed houses
- damaged palisade
- destroyed bridge

Unlock a **Settlement Status** panel.

Possible initial metrics:
- Food: Critical
- Medicine: Critical
- Shelter: Poor
- Tools: Critical
- Security: Poor
- Population: Low
- Bridge: Inoperable

---

### Q11 — Tonight's Meal [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

**Problem:** food is the first crisis.

**First guided action:** learn basic fishing at a nearby safe water source.

Then open a flexible **Food Supply** meter accepting fish, edible plants, hunted meat, and later cooked food.

**Visible change:** food appears on shelves/tables, villagers eat, starvation dialogue changes.

---

### Q12 — What Medicine Remains [MAIN] [RESTORATION] [PROFESSION]

**Problem:** Rhea used the last proper supplies on the player.

**Objectives:** medicinal herbs + clean water/cloth + basic preparation.

If Silverleaf was learned in Hollowpath, shorten the tutorial text and acknowledge prior knowledge.

**Visible change:** crude infirmary/herbalist corner becomes functional.

---

### Q13 — One More Night [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

**Problem:** communal shelter is barely weatherproof.

Player contributes a small mix of timber, fiber, and salvage cloth. NPCs perform the repair.

**Visible change:** patched roof and improved shelter.

**Stage unlock:** **HEARTHCROSS STAGE 1 — SURVIVING**.

---

## Optional Threads After Stage 1

### O01 — A Better Catch [OPTIONAL] [PROFESSION]
Deeper fishing: bait, rarity, water types, later weather/time effects.

### O02 — Useful Weeds [OPTIONAL] [PROFESSION]
Deeper foraging/herbalism: edible vs medicinal plants, quality, uncommon areas.

### O03 — The Empty Bed [OPTIONAL] [CHARACTER]
A resident tells of someone who left for supplies and never returned, leading to rescue/death/survivor content.

---

# PHASE THREE — HEARTHCROSS II: TOOLS TO REBUILD
## Stage 1 → Stage 2

### Q14 — Four Good Timbers [MAIN] [RESTORATION] [PROFESSION]

Preserve the current prototype's useful timber concept, but give it purpose.

**Problem:** Hearthcross needs structural lumber.

**Objectives:** receive/repair axe, identify harvestable trees, gather structural timber, deliver to work crew.

**Visible change:** lumber stacks and workshop scaffolding.

---

### Q15 — The Lost Tools [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

Recover the old tool crate from a failed supply/work run.

**Teaches:** off-road exploration, salvage, quest objects, optional encounters.

---

### Q16 — Stone Doesn't Grow on Trees [MAIN] [RESTORATION] [PROFESSION]

**Problem:** timber alone cannot rebuild the town.

**Objectives:** receive primitive pick, mine stone, gather basic ore.

**Teaches:** mining, node types, ore vs stone, tool progression.

---

### Q17 — A Place to Work [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

Supply workshop requirements. NPCs construct it.

**Build sequence:** ruined foundation → material piles → scaffolding → complete workshop.

**Unlock:** Carpenter/Workshop; construction recipes, tool repair, town projects, future housing vocabulary.

**Stage unlock:** **HEARTHCROSS STAGE 2 — WORKING**.

---

### Q18 — A Path Takes Shape [MAIN]

After meaningful contribution, the System has observed enough adaptation to stabilize a first Path.

Starter Paths:
- Vanguard
- Arcanist
- Shade

This happens after the player understands combat enough for the choice to mean something, rather than immediately after one chore.

---

# PHASE FOUR — HEARTHCROSS III: MAKE IT SAFE
## Stage 2 → Stage 3

### Q19 — Green Problem [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

Reuse existing name if desired.

Instead of simply killing three Mosslings:
- inspect damaged perimeter
- track nearby Mossling activity/nest
- clear enough threats to secure work area
- recover useful monster materials

**Visible change:** fence repairs begin.

---

### Q20 — The East Path [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

Workers cannot safely reach nearby resources.

Possible problems:
- hostile creatures
- fallen obstruction
- broken signpost
- alternate route discovery

Allow more than one solution where practical.

**Visible change:** NPC workers begin using the route.

---

### Q21 — Keep Watch [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

Supply timber, stone, and bindings/monster materials to restore palisade, gate, and watchfire.

**Unlock:** patrols, reduced immediate hostile presence, notice-board/contracts concept.

**Stage unlock:** **HEARTHCROSS STAGE 3 — DEFENDED**.

---

## Optional Combat/Hunting Threads

### O04 — Tracks in the Mud [OPTIONAL] [PROFESSION]
Introduce tracking/hunting as more than simply killing animals.

### O05 — The One That Comes at Dusk [OPTIONAL] [CHARACTER] [COMBAT]
First minor elite hunt tied to a recurring local predator.

---

# PHASE FIVE — HEARTHCROSS IV: BRING PEOPLE BACK
## Stage 3 → Stage 4

### Q22 — Too Few Hands [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

**Problem:** resources are no longer the only bottleneck. Hearthcross needs skilled people.

Introduce **Settlement Roles / Specialists**.

Potential missing roles:
- blacksmith
- herbalist
- fisher
- merchant
- scout
- cook
- carpenter
- trainer

---

### Q23 — Roadside Check-In [MAIN] [DISCOVERY]

Preserve existing quest name.

Travel to Wayfarer Camp and speak with Scout Mara.

Mara should provide multiple leads rather than one linear objective, such as:
- someone near Ironroot Quarry
- strange activity at Old Mossroad Ruins
- a fishing camp near Glasswater Pool
- a blocked supply trail

At this point, the player begins choosing order.

---

## Specialist Recruitment Branches

### Q24A — Iron and Ash [CHARACTER] [RESTORATION]
Recruit/recover a blacksmith near Ironroot Quarry or an abandoned smithing camp. The smith may require a safe road, recovered tools, or confirmed ore source.

### Q24B — Still Water [CHARACTER] [PROFESSION]
Recruit fisher/food specialist at Glasswater Pool. Could involve damaged boat, missing nets, hostile creature, or environmental problem.

### Q24C — What the Forest Knows [OPTIONAL] [CHARACTER] [PROFESSION]
Recruit herbalist through rare-herb, rescue, or stability quest.

### Q24D — A Place Worth Trading With [OPTIONAL] [CHARACTER] [ECONOMY]
Convince a merchant to return only after Hearthcross reaches credible food/security/workshop stability.

---

### Q25 — Fire Again [MAIN] [RESTORATION] [PROFESSION]

Restore the forge after securing smith, ore, stone, fuel/charcoal, and workshop capability.

**Build states:** cold ruin → cleared foundation → rebuilt chimney/workbench → first fire → operational forge.

**Unlock:** metal tools, equipment upgrades, refined resources, later bridge components.

**Stage unlock:** **HEARTHCROSS STAGE 4 — GROWING** once enough specialists/services are active.

---

# PHASE SIX — MOSSROAD VALE: REGIONAL RECOVERY
## Stage 4 → Stage 5

At this point, the game should largely stop behaving like a tutorial.

### Q26 — Old Stones [MAIN] [DISCOVERY]

Preserve existing quest name.

Scout Mara reports activity at Old Mossroad Ruins.

Player is told the problem/location, not every exact interaction.

Possible discoveries:
- regional history
- salvage
- old engineering records
- ancient materials
- lead to Barrowroot Ruins

---

### Q27 — The Quarry Road [MAIN] [RESTORATION]

Restore limited Ironroot Quarry output by solving collapsed access, creatures, abandoned equipment, workers, dangerous Stonebacks, flooding, or similar problems.

**Outcome:** periodic stone/ore becomes available to Hearthcross.

---

### Q28 — Glasswater [OPTIONAL/MAIN depending final bridge needs] [RESTORATION]

Establish sustainable fishery/safe fishing access so the player no longer manually supplies the town's basic food forever.

**Core progression principle:** earlier manual labor becomes automated by recovered infrastructure.

---

### Q29 — Briar Grove [MAIN or OPTIONAL ELITE]

Regional threat/boss such as Briar Troll blocks a needed route/resource.

**Reward:** rare material, morale, safety, bridge/forge material.

---

### Q30 — Below the Barrowroot [MAIN] [DUNGEON]

First dungeon should test:
- combat
- healing
- navigation
- resource management
- optional exploration
- environmental interaction

Reward a unique resource that matters to the world, such as bridge stabilization, forge upgrade, or Waystone restoration—not only a stronger weapon.

---

# RHEA CHARACTER ARC

### Beat 1 — Debt
Transactional relationship.

### Beat 2 — Useful
Rhea begins respecting the player.

### Beat 3 — Reliable
She trusts the player with real settlement problems.

### Beat 4 — Choice
The original debt is repaid. Rhea explicitly releases the player from it.

### Beat 5 — Partner
By bridgeworks, the player is treated as someone Hearthcross depends on, not someone who owes Rhea.

---

### Q31 — Paid in Full [MAIN] [CHARACTER]

Around late Stage 4/early Stage 5, Rhea acknowledges that the player has repaid far more than the supplies used to save them.

The player's motivation shifts from **I have to help** to **I want Hearthcross to survive**.

Possible reward: Hearthcross resident/citizenship status, reputation, future property eligibility.

---

## Housing Foreshadowing

### O06 — Four Walls of Your Own [OPTIONAL] [HOUSING PREVIEW]

Late starter region or shortly after bridge completion, allow the player to claim a ruined cottage/small plot/watchman's house.

Early functions:
- bed
- storage
- basic decoration
- trophies

Later functions:
- expansions
- crafting stations
- garden
- workshop
- companions/NPCs
- production

This should reuse the same construction language learned through Hearthcross.

---

# STAGE 5 — CONNECTED

Possible requirements:
- workshop active
- forge active
- food stable
- security stable
- quarry output active
- required specialists recruited
- first dungeon complete
- major regional threat handled

Visible effects:
- trade routes
- resource carts
- visitors
- wider notice-board contracts
- town no longer feels isolated

Then bridge repair becomes realistic.

---

# PHASE SEVEN — MOSSROAD BRIDGE
## Starter-region capstone

### Q32 — We Can Build It [MAIN] [CAPSTONE]

For the first time, the bridge is a realistic project rather than an impossible problem.

Requirements should mix:
- settlement readiness
- resource stockpiles
- specialist unlocks
- one/two special materials
- resolved regional threats

Possible materials:
- structural timber
- cut stone
- forged fasteners
- treated rope
- Barrowroot stabilizer/ancient component
- safe quarry route
- builder/blacksmith availability

---

### Q33 — Foundation [MAIN] [CAPSTONE]

Inspect unstable old supports, recover/craft reinforcement material, clear threats near crossing, assist specialists.

**Visible change:** worksite cleared and new support work appears.

---

### Q34 — Across the Gap [MAIN] [CAPSTONE]

Main span construction begins.

Player role should be solving final shortages, crafting a critical component, protecting crews, or retrieving a rare material—not hauling hundreds of identical logs.

NPCs visibly build the bridge.

---

### Q35 — The Last Piece [MAIN] [CAPSTONE]

Give the player one memorable final action:
- place keystone
- hammer final pin
- activate ancient stabilizer
- raise gate
- similar ceremonial/mechanical completion

The player should not personally build every part of the bridge.

---

### Q36 — The Road Opens [MAIN]

Hearthcross gathers at the bridge. Optional/recruited characters can appear based on completed quests.

Potential System message:

**REGIONAL ROUTE RESTORED**

**MOSSROAD VALE — PRIMARY RESTORATION COMPLETE**

Possible rewards:
- wider world access
- expanded Waystone network
- next main story arc
- reputation/title
- housing/property access
- shared-world transition if multiplayer uses this point

The player can look back at a thriving Hearthcross where ruins once stood.

---

## What Remains Optional

Do not require mastery of every profession.

Optional depth can include:
- advanced fishing
- hunting
- rare herbalism
- cooking
- elite monsters
- hidden ruins
- extra specialists
- full Hearthcross beautification
- rare building upgrades
- housing
- side-character arcs

The bridge requires broad literacy, not total completion.

## Systems Needed to Support the Questline

### Settlement State
Persist stage, metrics, structures, specialists, and resource thresholds.

### Building States
Each major structure needs ruined → under construction → functional → upgraded states.

### Town Contribution
Support both exact requirements and category/meter contribution, e.g. Food accepts multiple resource types.

### NPC Recruitment
Track discovered, recruited, arrived, service unlocked.

### Quest Graph
Support branching, concurrent quests, optional objectives, and prerequisite groups—not one active quest ID.

### World Persistence
Persist repaired routes, unique threats, construction stages, unlocked resource locations, and environmental changes.

---

## Quest UI Evolution

### Hollowpath
Explicit: **Gather the sturdy branch.**

### Early Hearthcross
Guided: **Hearthcross needs food. Try the stream east of town.**

### Mid Hearthcross
Problem-based: **Restore the workshop.**

### Late Hearthcross
Strategic: **Hearthcross needs skilled hands.**

### Mossroad Vale
Exploratory: **Scout Mara heard hammering near the old quarry.**

### Bridge Phase
Project-based: **Stabilize the Mossroad crossing.**

## Pacing Starting Point

- Hollowpath: 20–30 min
- Hearthcross Survival: 30–60 min
- Infrastructure: 45–90 min
- Security: 30–60 min
- Recruitment / first regional trips: 60–120 min
- Regional Recovery + Dungeon: 2–5 hours depending exploration
- Bridge Project: 45–90 min

Possible main-path starter-region target: **~6–10 hours**, subject to aggressive playtesting.

---

## Anti-Fatigue Rules

1. Never chain several identical gather quests.
2. Avoid 10 → 20 → 30 item-count escalation.
3. Resource requirements should create visible change.
4. Introduce no more than one major unfamiliar system at a time.
5. Give freedom before the player feels trapped.
6. Do not require profession mastery for main progression.
7. Reward curiosity from the beginning.
8. Count previously collected resources where reasonable.
9. Avoid artificial main-story construction timers.
10. NPCs increasingly handle repetitive labor.
11. Major milestones visibly alter Hearthcross.
12. Include conversation/exploration/judgment quests, not only combat/gathering.
13. Character quests should break up mechanical chains.
14. Bridge repair must feel earned, not grindy.
15. Rebuilding Hearthcross should be enjoyable even for players who dislike tutorials.

---

## Long-Term Payoff

Hearthcross teaches a controlled version of systems that can later become:

### Player Housing
Claim property, gather materials, build/repair, decorate, add functionality.

### Player Homestead
Workshops, gardens, storage, helpers, production.

### Player Settlement
Structures, specialists, supply chains, services.

### Player/Guild Town
Potentially multiple contributors, large resource projects, specializations, defenses, markets, transport links, civic/economic systems, settlement events.

Core Emberwild principle:

> Progress is not only something that happens to your character.
>
> Progress happens to the world around you.

---

## Recommended Implementation Pass

Before replacing the current prototype logic:

1. Lock Hearthcross starting layout.
2. Decide which buildings exist as ruins on arrival.
3. Choose mandatory specialist roster.
4. Decide which professions are mandatory only at literacy level.
5. Define settlement metrics.
6. Define building-stage assets.
7. Build a quest graph instead of one linear quest variable.
8. Lock Path-selection timing.
9. Decide whether housing begins before or after bridge completion.
10. Rewrite Hollowpath around crafting the first weapon.
11. Stop spawning completed Hearthcross buildings instantly.
12. Integrate Wayfarer Camp, Old Mossroad Ruins, Quarry, Glasswater, Briar Grove, and Barrowroot into the restoration economy.
13. Playtest for tutorial fatigue and dead time.
